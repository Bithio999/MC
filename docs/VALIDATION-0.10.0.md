## Build and gameplay

The build passes 11 JUnit tests and **58 required native GameTests**. Coverage includes:

- Secondary selection requiring terminal primary evolution, true awakening and base EP.
- A flat 10% native kill growth bonus retaining the primary alignment split.
- Secondary stages requiring fresh, cumulative EP from selection, plus native requirements.
- Both-final convergence, one-time energy reward, native saving/death cloning, unique profiles and inherited 10% growth.
- Native Godly/Ultimate classification, source replacement, presets and cooldown preservation.
- Worldline Sever's eight-target limit, initial magicule cost, held aim changes, paid repeat pulses, release cleanup and exhaustion cooldown.
- Event Horizon pulling outside its damaging core and bending hostile projectiles while preserving friendly shots.
- Resistance/nullification degradation through native gates, the non-Godly bonus and Godly-owner exclusion.
- Actual native damage against spatial nullification plus 90% spatial resistance while Aion is active, and nullification rejecting damage again after toggle-off; learned toggles remain intact.
- Proximity slow strengthening near the caster and expiring after the short lease.
- Reversal restoring recorded HP/SHP, rejecting unsafe returns without billing, and preserving energy/cooldowns.
- Verdict working alone or consuming its own Horizon, removing owned visuals, and clearing delayed effects on forgetting/lifecycle transitions.
- Native barrier ownership and recovery after quiet time.

All existing hunter, reincarnation, elemental attack, barrier, regeneration and otherworlder tests remain enabled.

## Minecraft client

A software-rendered development client checks the seven original-sized skill-category buttons, native blue panel fit, Godly title/list and right-side Secondary Race / Convergent Awakening navigation. An isolated development world verifies permanent secondary selection with confirmation, the `Sovereign Hunter/Slime` Status label, native character preview, and progression controls. The live 20M-EP character cannot evolve immediately; the first evolution succeeds at 22.5M, the next requires 25M, and the completed lineages awaken successfully through the right-side tab.

Worldline Sever is exercised through the actual native press/hold/release input path. Its synchronized visual follows the cast; releasing ends it and starts cooldown. The live view exposed an edge-on ribbon visibility problem, corrected with a volumetric beam, crosswise rings and an origin beside/ahead of the eye. The rings/core render in first person without the camera sitting inside the beam. Event Horizon's native gravity circle and domain render in the client.

Updated screenshots are included in the wiki. The cloud machine has no audio device, so audio is not validated. Remote multi-client play and long-term balance are not covered by these checks.
