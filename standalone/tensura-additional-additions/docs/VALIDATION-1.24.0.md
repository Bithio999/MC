# Additions 1.24.0 — Archive of Accord and one-time mentorship

The Archive is an ordinary vanilla jigsaw structure with a deterministic authored NBT template, existing Minecraft blocks, a dedicated start pool, Overworld biome tag and sparse random-spread structure set (spacing 128, separation 48, triangular spread). Its two-storey library includes shelves, reading alcoves, galleries, stairs, a slate roof, stained glass and a teaching dais. The template contains one persistent Elias entity. Normal single-pool placement handles entity rotation and finalization. Native generation requires depth 1: depth 0 produces no pieces in this Minecraft version, even though direct template placement can work. The regression fixture explicitly requires a non-empty native generated start.

Elias extends Tensura's OtherworlderEntity, uses its native player renderer and SmartBrainLib look behavior, and has an original classic 64px skin. He is a stationary, protected service NPC without a boss bar, combat loadout or Unique drops. A spawn egg supports creative/map setup; ordinary availability comes from the rare structure in newly generated chunks. Existing worlds retain all IDs and can discover archives by exploring new terrain. Native `/locate structure` and Marauder's Map use the registered structure ID.

The catalogue enumerates registered native Unique Skill types, including installed addons. It excludes Chained through a datapack-extensible tag, excludes non-Unique tiers, and consults Parallel World Jumper's single-player availability. Already learned skills cannot be bought; Chained and native acquisition checks remain in place. A taught skill starts learned, permanent and unmastered. The default payment is 1,000,000 permanent EP plus one Chromatic Orb; the EP value is configurable. There is no second native magicule acquisition charge. Raw permanent aura/magicule reserves pay proportionally; equipment/temporary capacity does not fund the bill. Current resources are clamped to the new capacities.

A UUID-keyed overworld SavedData ledger is the redemption authority, mirrored in the character's existing persistent addon data. Login, cloning and periodic reconciliation restore the marker after character-data resets; World Hop reconciles immediately before preparing travel. A traveling marker imports into the destination ledger. Losing the granted skill never restores the lesson. Normal acquisition rejection consumes neither payment nor redemption. Purchase execution is guarded against re-entry. The menu uses expiring player/NPC-bound nonces, rechecks life, visibility, dimension and distance, and rejects replayed or forged requests. A changed configured price requires a new confirmation.

Eight focused native cases cover exact proportional billing and Orb counts, no repeat purchase, gear/temporary capacity and creative mode, synthesis partners and excluded tiers, Chained/native unlock rejection, save/load and traveling marker import, source-skill loss and character-data reset, nonce ownership/replay and range, and rotated template/entity placement. The archive case also exercises native Structure.generate to catch empty jigsaw starts.

The actual-client review located and generated a natural archive, verified its NPC, opened the server-bound menu, searched and selected Prospector, canceled a confirmation, checked control bounds at normal and the maximum automatically supported GUI scale, then completed a real purchase and verified EP payment, Orb consumption and persistent redemption. Menu captures were inspected after correcting native hit bounds and disabling a background blur that otherwise affected already-drawn text. The inspected interior and menu/confirmation/completion captures are included with the wiki. Terrain-loading frames are excluded from published media. Review code is opt-in and restricted to disposable Archive Review saves.

Fresh-world regression setup was hardened after two existing fixtures assumed tracking within a fixed number of ticks: the High Realm density fixture now establishes a spectator observer for normal dimension tracking and loads its target chunks before creating its population, and the deployed-Vesper fixture waits for both the dummy and sword before its delayed-hit assertions. A review-only GameTestServer shutdown handler removes orphaned fake-player actors from level tracking and releases forced fixture chunks before bulk teardown. These helpers are excluded from production.

The baseline full run reported **402 required native gameplay cases passed**. The bulk harness then stalled during teardown and was terminated after its results were recorded. The teardown helper was subsequently extended to remove orphaned fake-player actors; an **8-case focused baseline run**, including a live tracked fake player, passed and exited cleanly, logging removal of that actor. The actual integrated-client purchase also saved and closed normally; its saved ledger contains the expected single permanent Prospector redemption.

The final **8-case mentor suite passed and exited cleanly on both supported profiles**: Tensura **2.0.1.3 / NeoForge 21.1.209** and **2.0.1.4 / 21.1.248**. Each teardown logged removal of its tracked fake-player actor. The second profile is an API/runtime compatibility check, rather than a full modpack playthrough. The final clean baseline production build passed **64 JVM tests** (no failures, errors or skips), and the distribution audit verified the 1.24.0 JAR, mentor classes, worldgen data, texture, metadata and absence of all review/temporary fixtures and bundled dependencies.
