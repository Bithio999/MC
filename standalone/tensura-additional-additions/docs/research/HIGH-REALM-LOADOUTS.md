# Native High Realm loadout audit

Reference runtime: Tensura Reincarnated **2.0.1.3**, CurseForge file **8793360**. Inspected registry fields and targeted bytecode/decompilations rather than assuming that learned spells automatically get used.

- `LesserDaemonEntity#getFightTasks` and its attack methods explicitly read Fire, Water Cutter, Wind Cutter, Stone Shot and Fire Ball from skill storage. `GreaterDaemonEntity#getFightTasks` similarly reads Fire Lance, Acid Shell, Icicle Lance, Tornado Blade, Thunder Orb, Stone Shot and Sleep Mist. Their native behaviours create `MagicCircle` entities, specify resource-cost pairs, play the registered GeckoLib attack animations and fire native magic projectiles. The addon grants those exact spells and leaves the behaviours intact.
- `IDaemon#getMagic` requires a learned spell, no cooldown and `canInteractSkill`; `canCastMagics` respects Anti-Magic. Merely adding arbitrary spells to other mobs would not teach their brains to cast them. The revised pools therefore include two native daemon casters.
- `WarGnomeEntity` and `UndineEntity` construct `ServerBossEvent` bars with boss music, despite living in the monster registry. Greater elemental spirits stay excluded. All selected ten mob types have `MONSTER` category, avoiding creature-category cap mismatches.
- `FighterSkill#onDamageEntity` and `BerserkerSkill` passive hooks check `isInSlot`. These Uniques are equipped in the native preset as well as mastered/toggled. Seer and Survivor have native toggle-based defensive hooks. Poison/Paralysis apply through native damage hooks when enabled. The addon adds no blanket barrier or infinite-regeneration layer.
- The casters use Mana and elemental Manipulation skills, whose native modifiers affect their attacks. Their fixed native AI warmups are not falsely presented as benefiting from Chant Annulment or Thought Acceleration.
- Spawn placement is material-sensitive. Lizardman's native predicate also requires below Y70 without open sky; Horned Bear/Blade Tiger use grass rules; ordinary species have additional configured rarity checks. Realm-local spawn events replace those restrictions with valid solid ground, native darkness/difficulty checks, world bounds and native obstruction checks. Spawn-pool weights control rarity inside this dimension; other dimensions, eggs, spawners and summons keep native rules. Other mods' explicit event failures are respected.
- `BrainUtils.setTargetOfEntity` synchronizes the SmartBrain attack memory as well as `Mob#setTarget`. The elite alert check runs once per second, respects ownership/alliance/line of sight and skips creative/spectator players and controlled mounts. Native movement and attacks remain responsible for combat.
- Versioned loadouts upgrade only eligible unowned saved elites. The upgrade does not reapply stat floors or refill HP/SHP/aura/magicules; replay does not stack toggle modifiers. Old vanilla elites are retained, and no new ones spawn.

See the runtime probes in `docs/review-high-realm/HighRealmLoadoutGameTests.java` and the release validation report for observed results.
