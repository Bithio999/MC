# Additions 1.24.1 — Archivist and archive redesign

Elias retains the native classic 64×64 otherworlder UV layout and player renderer. The skin is built directly on Kael's established pixel template: the same sparse eyes, coarse facial shading and small material highlights, with silver hair, a short beard and teal/ivory clothing. All base model faces are opaque, and the front hat layer cannot mask the face. The rejected high-resolution generated atlas is not used. The authoring script and building references are recorded in [the design notes](review-mentor/DESIGN.md).

The Archive template now has a slate gable over the main hall, a subordinate entrance gable, a chamfered copper-crowned tower and an east reading bay. Recessed glazing, projecting timber, shutters, sills and restrained weathering add depth. A staircase connects the gallery; a ladder joins the tower floors. Placement explicitly includes fence and iron-bar connections because known-shape jigsaw placement can skip neighbor updates. The 31×29×33 template includes one persistent Elias. The existing structure ID, biome selection, depth-1 jigsaw pool and sparse placement grid remain unchanged. Previously generated archives keep their buildings; new chunks receive the revised layout. Existing NPCs receive the texture update.

Anti-Skill is excluded through the same server-read tag used for catalogue eligibility and final purchase validation. The focused regression also attempts a direct purchase and checks that it is refused without consuming EP, an Orb or the one-time lesson. Already owned Anti-Skill is unaffected.

The baseline focused native suite passed all **8 required cases** and exited successfully. It covers native jigsaw generation, actual rotated template placement and the persistent protected NPC, along with purchase billing, persistence and session protections. The Anti-Skill test verifies both the catalogue and a direct server purchase attempt. The corresponding **8-case suite also passed and exited successfully on Tensura 2.0.1.4 / NeoForge 21.1.248**. This is an API/runtime compatibility check, not a full Neo Otherworld modpack playthrough; the baseline is Tensura 2.0.1.3 / NeoForge 21.1.209.

The actual client placed the revised native template on a level disposable review patch, waited for camera arrival, and captured the exterior, interior, gallery and Elias. The inspected published images use the final template, including supported roof ornaments and tower finial. The screenshot fixture never purchases a lesson or resets the redemption ledger. Previous generated atlases, terrain-loading frames and unsuccessful staging captures are excluded. Review-only code is opt-in and refuses non-review saves; the production distribution audit rejects its classes.

The final clean baseline production build passed **64 JVM tests**, with no failures, errors or skips. The distribution audit verified metadata, the native-sized skin, exact template/tag assets, and absence of review classes and bundled dependencies.
