Otherworlders

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Otherworlders are named characters that use Tensura’s native spawn, interaction, inventory and ownership systems.

Contents
  • Characters
  • Spawning and interaction
  • Skills and damage

Characters

Character Role Race Ability focus
Alma Expedition handler / researcher Awakened Hunter Analysis, perception and protection
Gemma Expedition smith Awakened Hunter Crafting, appraisal, physical work and flame control

Spawning and interaction

Both characters participate in the configured otherworlder distributions and can also be summoned directly. Their individual pages describe spawn commands, loadouts, starting energy and native behavior. Player-controlled skill modes do not imply autonomous NPC use.

Skills and damage

Each character’s Abilities table separates utility, defensive, healing and damaging effects. Native skills retain Tensura’s own behavior; their names do not place them in the addon’s Godly tier. Both characters also receive Hunter’s Recovery from their race.
