Heavenly Asura

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Heavenly Asura is the unique Convergent race formed from Ogre and Human ancestry. Ogre vitality and Human spirit form a single stable lineage.

Obtaining

Develop Ogre and Human as your two recorded ancestries, finish both evolution paths, and satisfy the Convergent Awakening requirements. Awakening grants a one-time configurable 25% increase to both base energy pools, then uses this race's own future EP allocation. It cannot be repeated to farm the bonus.

Traits

The fused body draws its intrinsic skill list and native race tags from the native final stages of both ancestral families. Previously learned intrinsic skills remain in native storage. Saved racial reactions and ticks from the actual two former stages continue to run. Body size does not combine the two ancestors' scales.

Shared additive attributes use 65% of each family's native final-stage bonus, plus 80 HP, 1,500 SHP, and an additional pair-dependent attack bonus. The total HP race bonus is capped at 1,000. These replace the old primary and secondary attribute contributions. Configured native race values affect the result.

Racial Ability

[Active - Racial Action] — Concordance Pulse

Damage type: None — healing and knockback. Damage ID: —. The pulse does not call an attack damage source; its visual is not elemental damage.

Restore 50% of maximum HP and 50% of maximum SHP, with no fixed healing-amount cap, and push eligible enemies within seven blocks away. Healing stops at each pool’s current maximum. The pulse costs 1,500 aura and has a 30-second cooldown. Its visual combines the two ancestral colors. Native sleep and movement-control restrictions prevent activation.

This action is shared by Convergent races; their ancestry-derived traits, icons and EP profiles distinguish each pair.

EP growth

Future native kill awards use 3.905 aura weight / 2.095 magicule weight. The inherited 10% EP gain bonus multiplies both weights by 1.1 without changing their proportions. Native gain attributes, penalties and caps still apply. Reversing the primary and secondary order produces this same race and growth profile.
