Origin Sovereign

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Origin Sovereign is Eschaton's boss-exclusive divine, spiritual lineage. It has no player evolution path, secondary-race selection or reincarnation roll.

Contents
  • Existence

Existence

The boss's one billion EP is stored in native aura and magicule maximums. His million HP and four million SHP are real damageable pools. The encounter retains native regeneration, resistance, elemental attributes and damage authority.

All three combat forms retain this race and its EP. Forms change the boss's anatomy and available patterns, rather than introducing three player races. See Eschaton's encounter guide.
