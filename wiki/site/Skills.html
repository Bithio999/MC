Skills

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Tensura Myths provides eight rollable Unique Skills, the race-exclusive Chained, five combination Ultimates, three Godly Skills, three aura recovery Extra Skills and Air Sovereignty, a Battlewill evolution.

Battlewills

Air Sovereignty evolves from mastered Air Float / Air Flight and enables aura-powered free flight. Use the normal Battlewill category and native learning system. See the Battlewill directory.

Using skills

Equip and cycle active modes through Tensura’s native Ability Selection and key bindings. The separate native toggle activates each skill’s passive. Uniques have two active modes, Ultimates have three, and each Godly Skill has four. Recovery Extra Skills are passive-only.

Scarlet Deluge remains a standalone water/control Unique. The synthesis reference explains exact acquisition and replacement behavior.

Ability reference

The ability index records every move’s damage type. Healing, barriers and utility list None; reflected shots retain their projectile’s own damage. Damage types explains native damage sources and status damage.
