Air Walk

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Air Walk is the Battlewill evolution of Air Float, called Air Flight (tensura:air_flight) in Tensura 2.0.1.3. Mastery of aura propulsion becomes controlled, unrestricted aerial movement. Its active uses the same native flight movement as Gravity Manipulation, powered by aura.

Obtaining

Master Air Float / Air Flight to unlock Air Walk as a learnable Battlewill in Tensura’s Ability Selection menu. Equip the new entry and train it through the normal learning system. Completing its learning enables its active; mastery is earned afterward. This follows the native Formhide → Haze progression pattern.

Existing players with mastered Air Flight receive the learning entry on login or the next progression check. Borrowed, temporary and sub-instance copies do not unlock it. Repeated discovery preserves the new art’s existing learning/mastery. The mastered predecessor remains usable and stays in its existing presets.

This is available to any lineage that meets the requirement. Chained permits it as a Battlewill and supplies its normal Battlewill learning/mastery bonuses. It does not require obtaining a magical skill.

Usage

Movement Native free flight, as used by Gravity Manipulation

Press the equipped ability to begin flying immediately. Use normal directional controls to move freely, jump to ascend and sneak to descend. You can hover in place without holding the skill key. Releasing the key keeps flight active. Vanilla flight controls handle takeoff and landing while the art remains enabled; press the art again to end its flight permission.

The default cost converts Air Flight’s 15 aura + 15 magicules into 30 aura, halved to 15 once Air Walk is mastered. The same mastery discount applies to activation and flying upkeep. Activation pays once. Each full second spent flying pays again; standing or otherwise retaining idle flight permission does not consume upkeep. Server changes to the predecessor’s configured aura/magicule costs change their sum here. The art consumes no magicules during activation, upkeep or native learning.

Running out of aura stops flight at the next upkeep payment. Magic Interference, movement restrictions, suppression and native area/boss ability bans also stop the art. Forgetting it or respawning removes its flight permission. Existing creative/spectator flight and flight supplied by native races, skills, transformations or equipment retain their own rules.

Two pale pressure waves and the native activation/deactivation sounds mark the change. There is no damage, elemental bonus, gravity attack or passive multiplier attached to this Battlewill.

Training

The art uses native Battlewill learning and 400 maximum mastery. Starting flight and successfully paying airborne upkeep award native mastery points. Chained’s enabled training bonus applies. Attempting to start flight without sufficient aura, or trying to activate an unfinished learning entry, does not grant free flight.
