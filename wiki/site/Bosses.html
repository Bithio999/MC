Myth bosses

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Myths are optional endgame boss encounters. Their EP, skills and spiritual health use Tensura's native systems. Prepare a wide arena and bring endgame mobility, healing and sustained damage.

Challenging a Myth

Ananke does not spawn naturally. Craft a Writ of Last Dawn and use it on a lodestone in open space on Easy, Normal or Hard. The writ is consumed only after a successful summon. A second ritual fails while another Ananke is within 128 blocks. Ananke has no player-EP entry gate.

For a managed multiplayer fight, an operator can prepare a native Tensura boss arena. This is an alternative to the survival ritual.

Bosses do not change terrain with their attacks. Their warning shapes matter: leaving a marked area is often safer than trying to absorb a Godly owner's damage. Individual boss articles list exact damage types, warning times and safe areas. Raw damage is before native elemental boosts, difficulty, armor, barriers and other combat handling.
