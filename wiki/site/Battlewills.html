Battlewills

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Battlewills are martial arts that use aura. Tensura Myths integrates its arts into Tensura’s native Battlewill category, learning and mastery systems.

Predecessor Evolution Requirement Function Damage type
None Schema 1,000 learning points; 2,000 mastery Build and toggle a six-effect Schema Tree None
Instant Move (tensura:instant_move) Instant Transmission Master the permanent predecessor; complete native learning Coordinate self-teleportation: 100 ticks, instant at mastery; 10 aura/block, 5 mastered None
Air Float / Air Flight (tensura:air_flight) Air Walk Master the permanent predecessor; complete native learning Press-controlled free flight using aura None

The predecessor stays available when its evolution is unlocked. Temporary or copied sub-instances cannot unlock permanent progression. Existing masters receive the learnable evolution automatically.

Chained allows Battlewills and provides its normal learning/mastery bonuses to them. Movement arts that deal no damage gain no additional damage from the Battlewill damage multiplier.
