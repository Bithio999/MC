Sovereign Rebirth

TensuraAdditionalAdditions · Skills

Sovereign Rebirth is an aura-powered Extra Skill granted by Heavenbreaker. It provides recovery inspired by native Infinite Regeneration.

Recovery

Damage type: None. Toggle through the skill menu. Missing HP and SHP every half second; funded protection against ordinary lethal HP damage.

30 aura per HP and 10 per SHP; 20 / 5 when mastered. Full applicable health costs nothing. Recovery is limited to the available aura budget; HP receives priority over SHP.

Only the strongest enabled aura-recovery tier heals. Full health costs nothing; recovery is limited by available aura and normal healing suppression.

All recovery tiers.
