Tensura Myths · Skills

Discontinued archive: Tensura Myths ended at 0.36.1. No further updates are planned.

All wikis · Tensura Myths

Eight skills join Tensura’s reincarnation pool. Chained is granted separately by Fettered Human.

Skill Role
Black Flame Black Flame is a Unique Skill inspired by Nu Udra. It uses visible oil tentacles to pull targets into burning ground.
Frostbound Colossus Frostbound Colossus is a Unique Skill inspired by Jin Dahaad. It combines avalanche impacts, ice capture, and a defensive carapace.
Mirrorbound Mirrorbound is a Unique Skill focused on splitting light attacks and timed projectile reflection.
Rune Smith Rune Smith is a Unique Skill focused on armed proximity trap and remote demolition.
Scarlet Deluge Scarlet Deluge is a Unique Skill inspired by Uth Duna. It combines travelling currents, aimed whirlpools, and native barriers.
Storm Crown Storm Crown is a Unique Skill inspired by Rey Dau. It combines ricochet lightning with an aimed movement art.
Threadweaver Threadweaver is a Unique Skill focused on single-target leash and spatial severance.
Verdant Keeper Verdant Keeper is a Unique Skill focused on allied hp/shp support and forward root control.
