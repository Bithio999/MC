Getting started

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

This guide covers installing Tensura Myths, rolling an apex skill, and beginning the Human hunter evolution branch.

Requirements

  • Minecraft 1.21.1 and Java 21.
  • NeoForge 21.1.209 or newer in the 21.1 line.
  • Tensura: Reincarnated 2.0.1.3, ManasCore 4.0.0.2, Architectury and Tensura’s required GeckoLib, SmartBrainLib and TerraBlender dependencies.
  • Tensura Myths 0.16.0 installed on both client and server.

Installation

Download the 0.16.0 addon from the repository’s releases directory. Replace older Wild Hunt / Tensura Myths jars; do not load two versions together. The addon jar is not a complete modpack.

First reincarnation

Complete Tensura’s ordinary reincarnation flow. All eight Myths Uniques are eligible starting rolls alongside the server’s configured pool. A particular apex skill is not guaranteed. Existing rolled skills remain valid after upgrading.

First hunter evolution

Stay Human, grow to 100K base EP, and defeat one eligible boss type. Select Awakened Hunter in the evolution menu or use /wildhunt evolve. Race progression and skill rolling are independent.

Choosing a playstyle

Choose Storm Crown for ricochets and mobility, Scarlet Deluge for currents and native barriers, Black Flame for visible tentacle pulls and burning ground, or Frostbound Colossus for avalanche impacts and ice capture.

Each skill has a separate toggleable passive in Tensura’s Ability Selection. Toggle it on for sustained benefits while paying aura upkeep. Mastery improves damage; EP does not supply an addon damage multiplier.

In-game help

/wildhunt guide explains progression. /wildhunt status shows base EP, distinct boss IDs, mastery and passive status. Command reference.

Beyond the hunter path

Use Tensura’s right-side Secondary Race / Convergent Awakening tabs and Skills → Godly → Ascension menus for Duo Races, Convergent Awakening, and Godly Skills. Use Skills → Ultimate → Synthesis for combination Ultimates. Aion and Thoth each have four active arts and a separate passive.
