Download Additions 1.19.2. Tensura Myths is not required. Do not install both addons together. Myths’ Godly skills, bosses, hunters and dual-race systems are not part of this addon.

Rare boss

Kael Veyr, the Starless Wayfarer is a rare 15M-EP otherworlder encounter in The High Realm.

Chromatic Orb: a one-time engraving upgrade crafted from High Realm shards and Kael’s Starless Core.
