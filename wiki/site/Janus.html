Janus, Lord of Crossroads is an Ultimate Skill focused on linked gates, opposing spatial blades and safe displacement.

Active abilities

Ability Damage type / base damage Cost Cooldown
Twin Gates None 4,000 aura placement / 400 transit 2 s
Parallax Snare Space elemental · 1,900 9,000 aura 8 s
Crossroads Edict Space elemental · 1,700 16,000 aura 18 s

Passive

  • Damage type: None — support / defense
  • Upkeep: 110 aura / second
  • Gain 35% movement speed, 30% attack speed, 0.5 knockback resistance and 100% Space boost. Threshold Dominion rewards movement and spatial offense.

Twin Gates

  • Damage type: None
  • Resource cost: 4,000 aura placement / 400 aura transit
  • Cooldown: 2 seconds
  • First cast costs 4,000 aura and places a gate at your feet and a second safe point up to 48 blocks along the aimed route. The pair lasts thirty seconds.

Parallax Snare

  • Damage type: Space elemental
  • Resource cost: 9,000 aura
  • Cooldown: 8 seconds
  • Raw damage: 1,900
  • Mark a point within 48 blocks. Opposing spatial blades close around a seven-block radius and strike after 0.8 seconds, for 1,900 raw Space damage.

Crossroads Edict

  • Damage type: Space elemental
  • Resource cost: 16,000 aura
  • Cooldown: 18 seconds
  • Raw damage: 1,700
  • Choose a field center within 32 blocks. Up to six visible enemies within ten blocks are displaced to their mirrored horizontal positions across the center.
