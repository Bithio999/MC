Boss-exclusive races

Origin Sovereign belongs to the final Myth, Eschaton. Eclipse Seraph belongs to Ananke. Neither is a player evolution, reincarnation roll or secondary-race option.

Human evolution explains boss credit, EP and mastery gates. Bonuses replace earlier stages.

Heavenly Restriction lineage

Start as Fettered Human, then evolve through Tempered Vessel, Unshackled, Heavenly Restricted and Heavenbreaker. Fettered Human grants Chained; later evolutions retain it without granting it again. The lineage uses a 90% aura / 10% magicule EP split. Fixed total EP, mastered Battlewills and Chained mastery govern this separate starting lineage. Unshackled, Heavenly Restricted and Heavenbreaker grant Hunter’s Recovery, Apex Renewal and Sovereign Rebirth respectively.

Secondary races

Choose a secondary lineage after completing the primary evolution path, reaching true awakening and 5M total EP. Each further secondary evolution requires native conditions and a new 2.5M-EP growth interval. A secondary adds 10% EP growth and displays alongside the primary in Status.

Convergent races

Convergent Awakening requires both lineages to finish evolving. Each unordered ancestral pair has one named race and its own growth profile, retaining the 10% bonus. Concordance Pulse is a shared non-damaging racial action.

The directory below groups all 105 races by ancestry; each race appears under both of its families. The awakening catalog lists each pair once with its growth weights.
