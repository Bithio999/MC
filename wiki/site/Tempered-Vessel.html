Tempered Vessel

From the unofficial Tensura Myths Wiki

Tempered Vessel is evolution 1 of four of the Heavenly Restriction lineage. A human-looking martial body exchanges magical growth for exceptional physical and aura potential through Chained, the Unique Skill inherited from Fettered Human.

Traits

You keep a human appearance and normal equipment. Each evolution replaces the previous stage’s bonuses.

EP growth splits 90% aura / 10% magicules. Chained multiplies later aura-capacity gains by 5× and blocks magicule-capacity gains; the blocked share is not redirected. A fresh Fettered Human starts with 1,500–2,000 aura and 50–100 magicules (1,550–2,100 EP before other rewards). Starting aura is not multiplied.

Chained governs true resource/learning restrictions, the martial toggle, mastered resistance degradation, in-slot dodge and Severance Rush. The same skill and mastery persist through the lineage.

Retained skill

Only Fettered Human grants Chained. This evolution preserves an existing Chained and its mastery but does not grant or regrant it. Chained supplies the lineage’s true restriction and martial abilities. Its active Severance Rush is an instant teleport that deals native Physical damage (minecraft:mob_attack). The race’s attribute bonuses are passive traits with no direct damage.

Evolution

Requires 100,000 fixed total EP, 1 mastered Battlewill, and 20% Chained mastery. All requirements must be met in Tensura’s native evolution menu.

Evolution checks total EP: effective maximum aura plus effective maximum magicules, as shown in Status. Chained’s bonus aura capacity counts. The milestones remain fixed constants, independent of the player’s EP when the lineage is selected.
