Overview

A human who advances through encounters with different bosses while preserving their original appearance. This is a native Tensura race in the alternate hunter branch.

Evolution requirements

Begin as Apex Hunter. Reach 2,000,000 base EP and defeat 5 different eligible boss types. Master at least one apex Unique skill as well. Boss eligibility and counting.

Racial attributes

Attribute Bonus at this stage
Maximum health +30 HP
Attack damage +6
Movement speed +15%
Knockback resistance +0.3

These replace the previous hunter stage’s racial modifiers. They do not stack with earlier stages. Skill mantles have separate temporary modifiers and remain subject to attribute caps.

Skills and progression

This stage unlocks the eligibility to evolve a mastered apex Unique into its matching Ultimate, provided you retain 2M base EP. Race evolution does not directly grant a Unique skill.

Notes

Boss credit survives death. Old ordinary-hunt counts from 0.1.0 are not converted into boss credit. Commands provide status and evolution controls.
