TensuraAdditionalAdditions · Equipment

Inverted Spear of Heaven is a rare ominous-vault reward. Its Nullification engraving can attack spiritual bodies, shatter barriers and briefly seal skills.

Chromatic Orb raises all eligible engravings by one level, beyond normal limits. Once per equipment piece; curses and blessings are excluded.

Vesper — Kael’s unique silver sword with the Afterwind engraving.
