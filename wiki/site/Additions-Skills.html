Skills

TensuraAdditionalAdditions · Skills

Chained is granted by Fettered Human and automatically evolves into Unbound when its requirements are met. Fusion temporarily combines bodies and skills with subordinates or consenting players. Prospector grants mining bonuses, ore vision and refining. Parallel World Jumper joins private single-player reincarnation rolls.

Battlewills include Schema, Air Walk and Instant Transmission. Aura recovery is granted by the lineage’s later evolutions.

Use Tensura’s normal Ability Selection menu. Ultimate synthesis combines fully mastered component skills after completed true awakening and a personal achievement. Each recipe has a permanent magicule cost; awakening bonuses apply before payment.

Ability directory.

Exponentiation raises supported beneficial values to ^1.2 while learned and equipped in your current skill preset. Movement is excluded.

Loan Shark lends skill copies, earns EP while they are used, and recalls or claims them through its report ledger.

Split Mind adds +9 learning/mastery gain, Thought Acceleration, Chant Annulment and automatic casting of one assigned learned magic.

Marauder's Map maps visited and unvisited terrain, tracks all online players, locates structures and saves private pins.

Critical Merchant compounds critical damage with chained coin flips and prepares ten guaranteed critical hits.

Ultimate synthesis

Hephaestus, Lord of the Forge combines fully mastered Prospector and Godly Craftsman after awakening and a personal forging achievement. No EP requirement. It adds Divine Forge, Living Arsenal and Magnum Opus.

Metis, Lord of Wisdom combines fully mastered Split Mind and Great Sage after completed true awakening and learning three magics. It adds a three-thread magical matrix (four at mastery), independent auto-casting and automatic magic-circle analysis. No EP requirement.

Tyche, Lord of Fortune combines Critical Merchant and Tuner: critical odds, a timed low-HP/SHP buff and damage-type-based full revival.

Laplace, Lord of Equations combines Exponentiation and Mathematician. Universal Formula inherits their benefits; Equation Editor changes verified native formulas with a live cost preview.
