# Skill visual effects

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Skills](Skills.md) · [Combat mechanics](Mechanics.md)

Tensura Myths gives each skill family an individual visual language, using articulated models, original inscription and surface textures, smooth keyframe animation and Tensura’s native magic circles. Effects are full-bright where appropriate and require no shader pack.

The formations combine detailed solid structures with flowing light: engraved storm conductors, coral crests, segmented oil tendons, hinged parchment grimoires, fate looms, recessed codices and reconstructed weapons. Ananke has layered armor, articulated feathers, ritual poses and an ascending phase animation. Alma and Gemma carry animated journals and smithing tools. The summoning scroll and Dawn Testament have three-dimensional item models.

## Display settings

Minecraft’s **Particles** setting reduces decorative motes, foam and cloud sprites. Primary attack paths, warning footprints, crystal geometry and gate frames remain visible even on **Minimal**. Distant effects use fewer curve segments. These settings do not change damage or server targeting.

Water Veil and Colossus Carapace have no constant custom particle cloud. Healing Extras pulse only when they actually restore HP or SHP. Native barrier rendering and the barrier HUD still belong to Tensura.

## Gallery

These in-game views show the geometry in an isolated development world. Some use a single staged visual to keep the shape unobstructed; they are not representations of damage, range or effect duration. The individual ability articles remain the gameplay reference.

### Storm Crown

[Storm Crown](Storm-Crown.md) — A coherent white-hot Railbolt core carries branching gold lightning and moving ion collars between its victims. Thunder Step leaves a tunnel of conductors and speed streaks along the travelled path; the lightning impact expands from the landing point.

![Railbolt’s branched lightning and ion collars](../media/visual-storm-crown.png)

### Scarlet Deluge

[Scarlet Deluge](Scarlet-Deluge.md) — Crushing Current is a curled, scrolling caustic water sheet with a bright foam crest. Scarlet Undertow forms a shallow raised vortex with four inward-flowing spiral ribbons, coral-colored fins and drifting foam. These surfaces mark the actual travelling wave and placed field; Water Veil adds no constant custom particle cloud.

![Scarlet Undertow’s layered water vortex](../media/visual-scarlet-deluge.png)

### Black Flame

[Black Flame](Black-Flame.md) — Oil Lash extends and recoils as a curved, tapered tentacle with suckers and molten seams. Each owned pool has an irregular dark oil surface. Ignition transforms that same surface into black flame silhouettes, violet inner fire, orange hot cores and rising soot; the surface fades when its pool ends.

![An ignited oil pool with dark flames and violet cores](../media/visual-black-flame.png)

### Frostbound Colossus

[Frostbound Colossus](Frostbound-Colossus.md) — Avalanche Crash erupts in faceted, veined ice spires with luminous edges and snowflake imprints. Glacial Prison arranges the crystals around its captured victim. The crystals remain visual geometry rather than placed blocks, and Colossus Carapace adds no constant custom particle cloud.

![Veined ice with luminous crystal edges](../media/visual-frostbound-colossus.png)

### Threadweaver

[Threadweaver](Threadweaver.md) — Loom Needle uses four braided strands, travelling pulses and an oriented silk-web anchor at its target. Sever Thread closes that web inward into a pink spatial snap. Its fine filaments remain distinct from Janus’s large architectural cuts.

![Braided silk and its target-facing web anchor](../media/visual-threadweaver.png)

### Rune Smith

[Rune Smith](Rune-Smith.md) — Runic Mine assembles two counter-rotating circuit inscriptions and six rising rune tablets. Command Detonation releases an expanding circuit front and luminous earth pillars. The armed mine has a stable footprint rather than a continuous shower of server particles.

![Runic Mine’s layered circuitry and rising tablets](../media/visual-rune-smith.png)

### Verdant Keeper

[Verdant Keeper](Verdant-Keeper.md) — Living Grove grows curved trunks, branching stems, roots and leaf clusters around a botanical ground diagram. Soft rising motes and an outward ripple accompany its healing field. Briar Grasp uses tapering, thorned vines around each caught enemy rather than a recolored grove.

![Living Grove’s branches, foliage and root network](../media/visual-verdant-keeper.png)

### Mirrorbound

[Mirrorbound](Mirrorbound.md) — Prismatic Lance has a white core, three refracted color strands and etched optical collars. Mirror Aegis uses independently orbiting faceted glass panels, luminous edges and inset mirror inscriptions. The formation follows the caster smoothly between server updates.

![Mirror Aegis’s faceted orbiting glass](../media/visual-mirrorbound.png)

### Vritra

[Vritra](Vritra.md) — Plasma Lance combines a continuous hot core, orange/violet helices and conductor collars; its delayed fuses converge into fire impacts. Thunderhead Furnace carries soft storm-cloud layers, periodic branching lightning and a suspended hot ring. Cataclysm Front warns with a fractured ground inscription before volcanic spires and flame jets erupt at the strike.

![Thunderhead Furnace’s storm cloud and conductor diagram](../media/visual-vritra.png)

### Demeter

[Demeter](Demeter.md) — Winter Orchard grows an ice-veined grove with curved crystal branches, snowflake tips, roots and flowing restorative ripples. Perennial Seed marks its actual recipient with a quiet crystal flower and opens a crystalline recovery bloom when triggered. Winter Harvest first shows its frost warning, then opens into a large eight-petalled crystalline bloom at the strike.

![Winter Orchard’s luminous veins and crystal branches](../media/visual-demeter.png)

### Janus

[Janus](Janus.md) — Twin Gate builds two freestanding frames with recessed, counter-rotating spiral apertures; entry and exit have distinct violet and gold accents. Passage leaves a short spatial transit streak. Parallax Snare closes opposing luminous cuts toward its warned center, while Crossroads Edict lays out a wide cartographic grid and cardinal anchors.

![Twin Gate’s freestanding frame and layered aperture](../media/visual-janus.png)

### Aion

[Aion](Aion.md) — Worldline Sever carries fine gold/violet helical strands and clock collars around its readable aim core. Event Horizon has a dark spherical core, tilted accretion optics, counter-rotating dials and a faint separate pull perimeter. Causal Reversal places a three-dimensional hourglass with flowing sand over its checkpoint clock. Genesis Verdict suspends twenty miniature black holes, bends each through a unique accelerating curve, and homes them continuously onto the moving target and resolves twenty individual detonations. Opaque speed-deformed cores sit inside broad luminous disks whose world orientation stays steady. Lensed rear-disk arches, photon rings, tapered wakes, large starbursts and expanding shock fronts distinguish the phases. Outer blast light is cosmetic; damage radii are documented in the ability article.

![Aion's twenty-singularity barrage](../media/aion-singularity-barrage.gif)

[720p native animation clip](../media/aion-singularity-barrage.mp4).

![Event Horizon’s dark core and tilted clockwork optics](../media/visual-aion.png)

### Thoth

[Thoth](Thoth.md) cycles Fire, Water, Lightning, Earth, Wind, Light and Space through flame crowns, caustic ribbons, branching bolts, stone tablets, wind helices, radiant spokes and spatial fractures. Its hinged parchment books, counter-rotating seals, spellbreak obelisks and held Mana Crucible alembic move together. Water represents the shared Water/Ice slot.

![Thoth’s held Mana Crucible formation](../media/thoth-mana-crucible.png)

## Combat readability

Bright cores mark beam paths, floor inscriptions preserve placed areas, and warned strikes build before their existing damage timings. Visual geometry has no collision and does not place terrain blocks. Server logic determines all hits, costs and cooldowns independently.

### Anansi

[Anansi](Anansi.md) uses articulated runic spider anchors and fine braided threads that match its placed network. Puppet Passage sends a woven transit along the route; Checkmate raises its fixed closing lattice and erupts along the consumed shape.

![Anansi's three-anchor runic network](../media/anansi-network.png)

### Aegir

[Aegir](Aegir.md) builds a circular caustic Mirrorsea and six floating glass lenses. Refracted Harpoon shows each converging water spear; Sovereign Breaker's narrow crest expands with its actual damage rim.

![Mirrorsea's caustic basin and glass lenses](../media/aegir-mirrorsea.png)

![Sovereign Breaker's expanding crest](../media/aegir-breaker.png)

### Lugh

[Lugh](Lugh.md) assembles ivory/gold armaments from solid, recessed rune tablets. Blade arcs, travelling lances and a descending crown identify its three forms. Grand Synthesis visibly assembles its archived forms in sequence. All-Skilled automatically scans nearby demonstrated techniques into Skill Bank, while Countermand locks a single rune tablet rather than surrounding the victim with a barrier.

![Lugh's three-technique synthesis formation](../media/lugh-synthesis.png)

## Ananke, the Unwritten End

An original six-winged ceremonial armor model has articulated feather spars, emissive violet cores and a rotating gold halo. Its [Moira rites](Moira.md) use different warning geometry: a locked fan, individually placed starfall seals, a hollow advancing wave and a dark sun above a real golden refuge ring. Cast poses ease into the animation rather than snapping. Geometry is rendered with native batched materials and distance detail limits.

![Ananke and the golden Last Dawn refuge](../media/ananke-last-dawn.png)
