# Sovereign Rebirth

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Skills](Skills.md) · [Ability index](Abilities.md) · [Damage types](Damage-Types.md)

**Sovereign Rebirth** is an aura-powered Extra Skill granted by [Sovereign Hunter](Sovereign-Hunter.md) or [Heavenbreaker](Heavenbreaker.md). It provides recovery inspired by native Infinite Regeneration.

## Obtaining

Evolve into [Sovereign Hunter](Sovereign-Hunter.md) or [Heavenbreaker](Heavenbreaker.md). Native racial learning grants and enables this tier. It is not a separate reincarnation Unique roll. The Heavenly Restriction lineage explicitly permits this racial grant despite Chained’s learning restriction; existing eligible characters receive missing tiers after upgrading.

## Usage

### Passive

[Passive - Toggle] — **Sovereign Rebirth**

**Damage type:** None. This skill heals; it does not create a damage source.

**Recovery:** Missing HP and SHP every half second; funded protection against ordinary lethal HP damage.

**Cost:** 30 aura per HP and 10 per SHP; 20 / 5 when mastered. Full applicable health costs nothing. Recovery is limited to the available aura budget; HP receives priority over SHP.

Only the strongest toggled aura recovery tier heals. Earlier tiers stay learned and can be used when stronger tiers are disabled. Toggle through Tensura’s normal skill menu; this recovery is not an active ability slot.

## Limitations

This tier can recover through ordinary Curse, Frost and strong Black Burn. Infinite Imprisonment, interaction suppression and severance limits still apply. Ordinary lethal-hit protection costs aura each time and preserves 1 HP; it cannot defeat invulnerability-bypassing damage, barrier bypass of at least 1.75, complete severance or spiritual-health depletion.
