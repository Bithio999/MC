## Obtaining

Begin as Apex Hunter. Reach 2,000,000 total EP and defeat 5 different eligible boss types. Master at least one Myths Unique skill as well. [Boss eligibility and counting](Evolution.md#distinct-boss-kills).

## Traits

| Attribute | Bonus at this stage |
| --- | ---: |
| Maximum health | +1,000 HP |
| Maximum spiritual health | +6,600 SHP |
| Attack damage | +6 |
| Movement speed | +15% |
| Knockback resistance | +0.3 |

These replace the previous hunter stage’s racial modifiers. They do not stack with earlier stages. Toggleable skill passives have separate modifiers and remain subject to attribute caps.

The HP bonuses across the branch are +100 / +500 / +1,000; SHP bonuses are +400 / +3,200 / +6,600. Each stage replaces the previous stage’s bonus.

## Racial Ability

**Damage type:** None — the race grants a recovery Extra Skill, not an elemental attack.

This stage grants **[Sovereign Rebirth](Sovereign-Rebirth.md)** as a native Extra Skill, at no permanent magicule acquisition cost. Earlier recovery tiers remain learned, but only the strongest toggled tier heals. Native racial skill learning enables the new tier automatically.

Base energy uses two thirds aura and one third magicules. Default-alignment kill EP retains Tensura’s native 2:1 split.


At 2M total EP, any race can combine two specific mastered Uniques through [Skill synthesis](Skill-Synthesis.md). Race evolution does not directly grant a Unique skill.
