# Race directory

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Races](Races.md) · [Ability index](Abilities.md) · [Damage types](Damage-Types.md)

**Races** are organized into the Human hunter branch, independent secondary lineages and unified Convergent races. The addon preserves the native race system and requirements.

## Boss-exclusive races

[Origin Sovereign](Origin-Sovereign.md) belongs to the final Myth, [Eschaton](Eschaton.md). [Eclipse Seraph](Eclipse-Seraph.md) belongs to Ananke. Neither is a player evolution, reincarnation roll or secondary-race option.

[Human evolution](Evolution.md) explains boss credit, EP and mastery gates. Bonuses replace earlier stages.

## Secondary races

[Choose a secondary lineage](Duo-Races.md) after completing the primary evolution path, reaching true awakening and 5M base EP. Each further secondary evolution requires native conditions and a new 2.5M-EP growth interval. A secondary adds 10% EP growth and displays alongside the primary in Status.

## Convergent races

[Convergent Awakening](Convergent-Awakening.md) requires both lineages to finish evolving. Each unordered ancestral pair has one named race and its own growth profile, retaining the 10% bonus. Concordance Pulse is a shared non-damaging racial action.

The directory below groups all 105 races by ancestry; each race appears under both of its families. The [awakening catalog](Convergent-Awakening.md#race-catalog) lists each pair once with its growth weights.
