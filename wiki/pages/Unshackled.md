# Unshackled

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Race directory](Races.md) · [Chained](Chained.md)

**Unshackled** is evolution 2 of four of the **Heavenly Restriction** lineage. A human-looking martial body exchanges magical growth for exceptional physical and aura potential through **Chained**, the Unique Skill inherited from Fettered Human.

## Traits

The race preserves the player’s appearance and equipment. Stage bonuses replace the preceding stage’s bonuses. Its EP growth splits **9/10 to aura and 1/10 to magicules**. Chained multiplies later aura-capacity gains by 5 and blocks future maximum-magicule gains; the blocked share is not reassigned. A newly reincarnated Fettered Human rolls **1,500–2,000 base aura** and **50–100 base magicules**. Chained leaves that starting aura unmultiplied, so starting EP is **1,550–2,100**, before any separate reincarnation rewards. The actual starting roll is preserved through evolution. A secondary race’s 10% EP bonus preserves these proportions; Convergent races use their own growth profiles.

[Chained](Chained.md) governs true resource/learning restrictions, the martial toggle, mastered resistance degradation, in-slot dodge and Severance Rush. The same skill and mastery persist through the lineage.

## Retained skill

Only Fettered Human grants [Chained](Chained.md). This evolution preserves an existing Chained and its mastery but does not grant or regrant it. Chained supplies the lineage’s true restriction and martial abilities. Its active [Severance Rush](Chained.md#severance-rush) is an instant teleport that deals native **Aura slash / Battlewill** damage (`tensura:aura_slash`). The race’s attribute bonuses are passive traits with no direct damage.

## Evolution

Requires **500,000 fixed total EP**, **2 mastered Battlewills**, and **40% Chained mastery**. All requirements must be met in Tensura’s native evolution menu.

Evolution checks **total EP**: effective maximum aura plus effective maximum magicules, as shown in Status. Chained’s bonus aura capacity counts. The milestones remain fixed constants, independent of the player’s EP when the lineage is selected.
