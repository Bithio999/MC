# Otherworlders

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Otherworlders](Otherworlders.md) · [Ability index](Abilities.md) · [Damage types](Damage-Types.md)

**Otherworlders** are named characters that use Tensura’s native spawn, interaction, inventory and ownership systems.

## Characters

| Character | Role | Race | Ability focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Alma](Alma.md) | Expedition handler / researcher | Awakened Hunter | Analysis, perception and protection |
| [Gemma](Gemma.md) | Expedition smith | Awakened Hunter | Crafting, appraisal, physical work and flame control |

## Spawning and interaction

Both characters participate in the configured otherworlder distributions and can also be summoned directly. Their individual pages describe spawn commands, loadouts, starting energy and native behavior. Player-controlled skill modes do not imply autonomous NPC use.

## Skills and damage

Each character’s Abilities table separates utility, defensive, healing and damaging effects. Native skills retain Tensura’s own behavior; their names do not place them in the addon’s Godly tier. Both characters also receive [Hunter’s Recovery](Hunters-Recovery.md) from their race.
