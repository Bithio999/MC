# Getting started

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Skills](Skills.md) · [Evolution](Evolution.md) · [Mechanics](Mechanics.md) · [Commands](Commands.md)

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1 and Java 21.
- NeoForge 21.1.209 or newer in the 21.1 line.
- Tensura: Reincarnated 2.0.1.3, ManasCore 4.0.0.2, Architectury and Tensura’s required GeckoLib, SmartBrainLib and TerraBlender dependencies.
- Wild Hunt 0.4.0 installed on both client and server.

## Installation

Download the 0.4.0 addon from the repository’s `releases` directory. Replace older Wild Hunt jars; do not load two versions together. The addon jar is not a complete modpack.

## First reincarnation

Complete Tensura’s ordinary reincarnation flow. The four apex Uniques are eligible starting rolls alongside the server’s configured pool. A particular apex skill is not guaranteed. Existing rolled skills remain valid after upgrading.

## First hunter evolution

Stay Human, grow to 100K base EP, and defeat one eligible boss type. Select Awakened Hunter in the evolution menu or use `/wildhunt evolve`. Race progression and skill rolling are independent.

## Choosing a playstyle

Choose [Storm Crown](Storm-Crown.md) for precision and charge management, [Scarlet Deluge](Scarlet-Deluge.md) for movement control, [Black Flame](Black-Flame.md) for oil placement and detonations, or [Frostbound Colossus](Frostbound-Colossus.md) for ground ruptures and projectile defense.

## In-game help

`/wildhunt guide` explains progression. `/wildhunt status` shows base EP, distinct boss IDs, mastery and art resources. [Command reference](Commands.md).
