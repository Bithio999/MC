**Jormungandr** is the unique Convergent race formed from **Giant** and **Lizardman** ancestry. Giant vitality and Lizardman spirit form a single stable lineage.

## Obtaining

Finish both ancestral evolution paths, become a True Hero or True Demon Lord, and meet the fixed EP gate shown in the **Convergent Awakening** tab (at least 10M EP). Awakening grants a one-time +25% to both base energy pools and retains learned skills and the +10% EP gain bonus.

## Racial ability

**Concordance Pulse — no damage.** Heal 50% maximum HP and SHP, without a fixed amount cap, and push nearby enemies. Cost: **1,500 aura**. Cooldown: **30 seconds**. Healing stops at your current maximums.

Traits combine both final ancestries. [Awakening details and shared traits](Convergent-Awakening.md).
