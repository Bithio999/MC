# Heavenly Restricted

[Main Page](../README.md) · [Race directory](Races.md) · [Chained](Chained.md)

**Heavenly Restricted** is evolution 3 of four of the **Heavenly Restriction** lineage. A human-looking martial body exchanges magical growth for exceptional physical and aura potential through **Chained**, the Unique Skill inherited from Fettered Human.

## Traits

You keep a human appearance and normal equipment. Each evolution replaces the previous stage’s bonuses.

EP growth splits **90% aura / 10% magicules**. [Chained](Chained.md#heavenly-restriction) multiplies later aura-capacity gains by **5×** and blocks magicule-capacity gains; the blocked share is not redirected. A fresh Fettered Human starts with **1,500–2,000 aura** and **50–100 magicules** (**1,550–2,100 EP** before other rewards). Starting aura is not multiplied.

[Chained](Chained.md) governs true resource/learning restrictions, the martial toggle, mastered resistance degradation, in-slot dodge and Severance Rush. The same skill and mastery persist through the lineage.

## Retained skill

Only Fettered Human grants [Chained](Chained.md). This evolution preserves an existing Chained and its mastery but does not grant or regrant it. Chained supplies the lineage’s true restriction and martial abilities. Its active [Severance Rush](Chained.md#severance-rush) is an instant teleport that deals native **Aura slash / Battlewill** damage (`tensura:aura_slash`). The race’s attribute bonuses are passive traits with no direct damage.

## Aura recovery

This stage grants and enables [Apex Renewal](Apex-Renewal.md) through native racial learning, without a magicule acquisition cost. Earlier aura-recovery tiers remain learned; only the strongest toggled tier heals. These racial grants are specifically permitted by Chained. Existing characters receive missing eligible tiers automatically after upgrading.

## Evolution

Requires **2,000,000 fixed total EP**, **3 mastered Battlewills**, and **60% Chained mastery**. All requirements must be met in Tensura’s native evolution menu.

Evolution checks **total EP**: effective maximum aura plus effective maximum magicules, as shown in Status. Chained’s bonus aura capacity counts. The milestones remain fixed constants, independent of the player’s EP when the lineage is selected.
