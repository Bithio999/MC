## Characteristics

Ananke's initialization assigns this race through native RaceAPI and sets 30M base EP, 48,000 HP and 120,000 SHP. The boss's encounter attributes belong to the entity; this race is not a way for players to obtain those stats. There are no next or previous evolutions and no fusion catalog entry for this lineage.

Its even aura/magicule base split supplies the boss's native energy storage. Combat arts consume magicules and never use EP as a damage multiplier.
