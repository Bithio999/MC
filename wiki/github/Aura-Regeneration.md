# Aura recovery skills

> **Discontinued archive:** Tensura Myths ended at 0.36.1. No further updates are planned.

[All wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Home) · [Tensura Myths](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Tensura-Myths)

Toggle a recovery skill to heal with aura. Only your strongest toggled tier works; full reserves cost nothing.

## Hunter’s Recovery

Restore 4 HP per second, or 8 mastered, at 20 aura per base HP.


## Apex Renewal

Restore missing HP every half second. Cost: 30 aura per base HP, or 20 mastered.


## Sovereign Rebirth

Restore HP and SHP every half second. Cost: 30 aura per HP / 10 per SHP, or 20 / 5 mastered. Can prevent ordinary fatal damage while funded; native bypass and suppression rules still apply.


## Obtaining

Granted by the relevant hunter or Heavenly Restriction evolutions. Earlier recovery skills remain available, but do not heal in parallel.
