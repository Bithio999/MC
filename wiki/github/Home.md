# Tensura addon wikis

**TensuraAdditionalAdditions is the active project.** The Myths wiki is preserved as a discontinued archive. Each section has separate navigation, skills, progression, pictures and videos.

| Wiki | Status | Version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[TensuraAdditionalAdditions](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/TensuraAdditionalAdditions)** | Active — Heavenly Restriction, Battlewills, skills, gear, world travel and The High Realm | 1.30.0 |
| **[Tensura Myths](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Tensura-Myths)** | Discontinued — archived races, skills, dual races, bosses and otherworlders | Final: 0.36.1 |

Both require **Tensura: Reincarnated**, Minecraft **1.21.1** and **NeoForge**. They are alternative distributions; do not install them together.

Entries cover use, obtainment, damage types, costs, cooldowns and necessary limits. Values are defaults before defenses and other bonuses. Development notes and change history live outside the gameplay entries.

## Offline edition

[Download the wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/raw/refs/heads/main/releases/tensura-addon-wikis.zip), extract the ZIP, and open `index.html`. Search is scoped to the selected addon; the main page searches both sections. Pictures and linked videos work offline.

AdditionalAdditions includes an **F8** [in-game guide](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-In-Game-Guide).
