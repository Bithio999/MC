Do not install both addons together. Myths’ Godly skills, bosses, hunters and dual-race systems are not part of this addon.

## Rare boss

[Kael Veyr, the Starless Wayfarer](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Kael-Veyr) is a rare 15M-EP otherworlder encounter in The High Realm.

[Chromatic Orb](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Chromatic-Orb): a one-time engraving upgrade crafted from High Realm shards and Kael’s Starless Core.

## Unique Skill mentorship

Find **Elias, the Archivist** in the rare [Archive of Accord](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Archive-of-Accord). Each player may buy one permanent, unmastered Unique Skill for **1,000,000 permanent EP and one Chromatic Orb** by default.
