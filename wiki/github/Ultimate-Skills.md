# Ultimate Skills

[Main Page](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [Skills](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Skills) · [Ability index](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Abilities) · [Damage types](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types)

**Ultimate Skills** combine two mastered Uniques at **2M base EP**, through **Skills → Ultimate → Synthesis**. The addon provides five distinct kits; any race can synthesize them.

## Obtaining

See [Skill synthesis](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Skill-Synthesis) for the recipes and menu. Both mastered ingredients are combined. Unrelated skills are retained; presets and the longest remaining cooldown carry over. Synthesis does not spend EP or require Sovereign Hunter.

## Usage

Three active modes use aura; the separate native toggle charges aura upkeep. Damage uses a mastery multiplier up to 1.5×, never EP. Their active arts carry native level-1 resistance penetration, while native attribute defenses, nullification fallback, barriers, hit immunity and authority retain their independent rules. Ordinary elemental boosts use their strongest source per element. These skills do not reuse their ingredients’ active modes. Each article lists raw damage and native damage IDs per ability.

## Evolution

Master one of these Ultimates, become a True Hero or True Demon Lord and reach 12M base EP to obtain [Aion](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Aion), or additionally combine a mastered [Rune Smith](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Rune-Smith) for [Thoth](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Thoth), or combine a mastered [Threadweaver](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Threadweaver) for [Lugh](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Lugh).

## Boss-intrinsic Ultimates

[Moira, Lord of Finality](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Moira) belongs to [Ananke](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Ananke). Its four native Ultimate rites are encounter mechanics, with no player roll, synthesis or slotting route. It is separate from the five playable combinations above.
