# Battlewills

[Main Page](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [Skills](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Skills) · [Ability index](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Abilities)

**Battlewills** are martial arts that use aura. Tensura Myths integrates its arts into Tensura’s native Battlewill category, learning and mastery systems.

| Predecessor | Evolution | Requirement | Function | Damage type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Air Float / Air Flight (`tensura:air_flight`) | [Air Sovereignty](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Air-Sovereignty) | Master the permanent predecessor; complete native learning | Press-controlled free flight using aura | None |

The predecessor stays available when its evolution is unlocked. Temporary or copied sub-instances cannot unlock permanent progression. Existing masters receive the learnable evolution automatically.

[Chained](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Chained) allows Battlewills and provides its normal learning/mastery bonuses to them. Movement arts that deal no damage gain no additional damage from the Battlewill damage multiplier.
