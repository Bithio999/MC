## Learn

Master your permanent Air Float, then complete the new learning entry in the Battlewill menu.


## Use

Press to enable free flight using aura. Press again to stop; stopping is free even with empty aura.

Cost: 30 aura to activate and 30 aura per second actually flying. Mastery halves both to 15. No magicule cost or cooldown.
