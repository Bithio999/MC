## Obtaining

Evolve into [Awakened Hunter](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Awakened-Hunter). Native racial learning grants and enables this tier. It is not a separate reincarnation Unique roll.

## Usage

### Passive

[Passive - Toggle] — **Hunter’s Recovery**

**Damage type:** None. This skill heals; it does not create a damage source.

**Recovery:** 4 HP each second, or 8 HP when mastered.

**Cost:** 20 aura per HP. Full applicable health costs nothing. Recovery is limited to the available aura budget; HP receives priority over SHP.

Only the strongest toggled aura recovery tier heals. Earlier tiers stay learned and can be used when stronger tiers are disabled. Toggle through Tensura’s normal skill menu; this recovery is not an active ability slot.

## Limitations

Ordinary healing suppression, including Frost and Curse, prevents this tier from recovering HP. Native interaction suppression and severance limits also apply.
