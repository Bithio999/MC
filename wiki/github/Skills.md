# Skills

[Main Page](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [Skills](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Skills) · [Ability index](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Abilities) · [Damage types](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types)

**Skills** are organized by tier and acquisition method. The addon provides four Unique Skills, four Ultimate Skills, one Godly skill and three aura recovery Extra Skills.

## Skill tiers

| Tier | Skills | Obtaining | Resource |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Unique](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Unique-Skills) | Storm Crown, Scarlet Deluge, Black Flame, Frostbound Colossus | Reincarnation starting-skill rolls | Aura |
| [Ultimate](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Ultimate-Skills) | Raijin, Varuna, Agni, Ymir | Matching mastered Unique; Sovereign Hunter; 2M base EP | Aura |
| [Godly](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Godly) | Aion, God of Causality | Mastered Myths Ultimate; True Hero / True Demon Lord; 12M base EP | Magicules |
| [Extra](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Aura-Regeneration) | Hunter’s Recovery, Apex Renewal, Sovereign Rebirth | Hunter race evolution | Aura |

## Individual abilities

The [ability index](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Abilities) lists every active art, toggleable passive and shared racial action, with a link to its description and its damage type. Healing and utility arts are labeled **None**. [Damage types](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types) explains direct elemental hits, status damage, armor and barriers.

## Using skills

Equip active modes and cycle them through Tensura’s native ability interface. Passives use the native toggle. Each apex Unique has two active modes; its Ultimate has three. Aion has four active modes and a separate passive. Recovery Extra Skills are passive-only.
