# Race directory

[Main Page](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [Races](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Races) · [Ability index](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Abilities) · [Damage types](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types)

**Races** are organized into the Human hunter branch, independent secondary lineages and unified Convergent races. The addon preserves the native race system and requirements.

## Hunter races

| Race | Stage | HP / SHP racial bonus | Recovery Extra Skill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Awakened Hunter](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Awakened-Hunter) | 1 | +100 / +400 | [Hunter’s Recovery](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Hunters-Recovery) |
| [Apex Hunter](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Apex-Hunter) | 2 | +500 / +3,200 | [Apex Renewal](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Apex-Renewal) |
| [Sovereign Hunter](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Sovereign-Hunter) | 3 | +1,000 / +6,600 | [Sovereign Rebirth](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Sovereign-Rebirth) |

[Human evolution](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Evolution) explains boss credit, EP and mastery gates. Bonuses replace earlier stages.

## Secondary races

[Choose a secondary lineage](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Duo-Races) after completing the primary evolution path, reaching true awakening and 5M base EP. Each further secondary evolution requires native conditions and a new 2.5M-EP growth interval. A secondary adds 10% EP growth and displays alongside the primary in Status.

## Convergent races

[Convergent Awakening](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Convergent-Awakening) requires both lineages to finish evolving. Each unordered ancestral pair has one named race and its own growth profile, retaining the 10% bonus. Concordance Pulse is a shared non-damaging racial action.

The directory below groups all 105 races by ancestry; each race appears under both of its families. The [awakening catalog](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Convergent-Awakening#race-catalog) lists each pair once with its growth weights.
