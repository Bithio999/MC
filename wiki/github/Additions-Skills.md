# Skills

[All wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Home) · [TensuraAdditionalAdditions](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/TensuraAdditionalAdditions)

[Chained](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Chained) is granted by Fettered Human and automatically evolves into [Unbound](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Unbound) when its requirements are met. [Fusion](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Fusion) temporarily combines bodies and skills with subordinates or consenting players. [Prospector](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Prospector) grants mining bonuses, ore vision and refining. [Parallel World Jumper](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Parallel-World-Jumper) joins private single-player reincarnation rolls.

[Battlewills](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Battlewills) include Schema, Air Walk and Instant Transmission. [Aura recovery](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Aura-Regeneration) is granted by the lineage’s later evolutions.

Use Tensura’s normal Ability Selection menu. Ultimate synthesis combines fully mastered component skills after completed true awakening and a personal achievement. Each recipe has a permanent magicule cost; awakening bonuses apply before payment.

[Ability directory](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Abilities).

[Exponentiation](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Exponentiation) raises supported beneficial values to ^1.2 while learned and equipped in your current skill preset. Movement is excluded.

[Loan Shark](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Loan-Shark) lends skill copies, earns EP while they are used, and recalls or claims them through its report ledger.

[Split Mind](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Split-Mind) adds +9 learning/mastery gain, Thought Acceleration, Chant Annulment and automatic casting of one assigned learned magic.

[Marauder's Map](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Marauders-Map) maps visited and unvisited terrain, tracks all online players, locates structures and saves private pins.

[Critical Merchant](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Critical-Merchant) compounds critical damage with chained coin flips and prepares ten guaranteed critical hits.

## Ultimate synthesis

[Hephaestus, Lord of the Forge](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Hephaestus) combines fully mastered Prospector and Godly Craftsman after awakening and a personal forging achievement. No EP requirement. It adds Divine Forge, Living Arsenal and Magnum Opus.

[Metis, Lord of Wisdom](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Metis) combines fully mastered Split Mind and Great Sage after completed true awakening and learning three magics. It adds a three-thread magical matrix (four at mastery), independent auto-casting and automatic magic-circle analysis. No EP requirement.

[Tyche, Lord of Fortune](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Tyche) combines Critical Merchant and Tuner: critical odds, a timed low-HP/SHP buff and damage-type-based full revival.

[Laplace, Lord of Equations](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Laplace) combines Exponentiation and Mathematician. Universal Formula inherits their benefits; Equation Editor changes verified native formulas with a live cost preview.
