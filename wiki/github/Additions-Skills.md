# Skills

[All wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [TensuraAdditionalAdditions](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/TensuraAdditionalAdditions)

[Chained](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Chained) is granted by Fettered Human and automatically evolves into [Unbound](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Unbound) when its requirements are met. [Fusion](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Fusion) temporarily combines bodies and skills with subordinates or consenting players. [Prospector](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Prospector) grants mining bonuses, ore vision and refining. [Parallel World Jumper](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Parallel-World-Jumper) joins private single-player reincarnation rolls.

[Battlewills](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Battlewills) include Schema, Air Walk and Instant Transmission. [Aura recovery](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Aura-Regeneration) is granted by the lineage’s later evolutions.

Use Tensura’s normal Ability Selection menu. This addon has no Godly skill tier, hunter branch or skill-synthesis system.

[Ability directory](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Abilities).

[Exponentiation](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Exponentiation) squares supported beneficial values while learned and equipped in your current skill preset. Movement is excluded.

[Loan Shark](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Loan-Shark) lends skill copies, earns EP while they are used, and recalls or claims them through its report ledger.

[Split Mind](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Split-Mind) adds +9 learning/mastery gain, Thought Acceleration, Chant Annulment and automatic casting of one assigned learned magic.

[Marauder's Map](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Marauders-Map) maps explored terrain, locates world-generation structures and saves private custom pins.
