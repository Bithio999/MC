# Skills

[All wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [TensuraAdditionalAdditions](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/TensuraAdditionalAdditions)

[Chained](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Chained) is granted by Fettered Human and automatically evolves into [Unbound](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Unbound) when its requirements are met. [Fusion](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Fusion) temporarily combines bodies and skills with subordinates or consenting players. [Parallel World Jumper](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Parallel-World-Jumper) joins private single-player reincarnation rolls.

[Battlewills](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Battlewills) include Schema, Air Walk and Instant Transmission. [Aura recovery](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Additions-Aura-Regeneration) is granted by the lineage’s later evolutions.

Use Tensura’s normal Ability Selection menu. This addon has no Godly skill tier, hunter branch or skill-synthesis system.
