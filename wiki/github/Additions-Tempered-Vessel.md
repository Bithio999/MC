# Tempered Vessel

[All wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Home) · [TensuraAdditionalAdditions](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/TensuraAdditionalAdditions)

**Tempered Vessel** is evolution 1 of four of the **Heavenly Restriction** lineage. A human-looking martial body exchanges magical growth for exceptional physical and aura potential through **Chained**, the Unique Skill inherited from Fettered Human.

[Heavenly Restriction progression](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Fettered-Human). Racial bonuses replace earlier stages; they do not stack.
