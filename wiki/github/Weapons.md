# Weapons

[Main Page](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [Damage types](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types)

Tensura Myths weapons use Minecraft's equipment and enchantment system with Tensura's native engravings.

| Weapon | Damage type | Attack damage / speed | Obtaining |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Inverted Spear of Heaven](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Inverted-Spear-of-Heaven) | Physical | 80 / 1.6; matches Hihiirokane Sword | 2% additional ominous vault reward |

The [Nullification](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Nullification) engraving breaks barriers, removes skill status effects and prevents skill activation for two seconds.

[Chained's Severance Rush](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Chained#severance-rush) and [Unbound's Counter](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Unbound#counter) include main-hand weapon damage, enchantments and engraving hit effects. Their aura-based damage is added to the weapon contribution before their normal Physical modifiers and defenses.
