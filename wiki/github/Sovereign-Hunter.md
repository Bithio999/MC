## Overview

A human who advances through encounters with different bosses while preserving their original appearance. This is a native Tensura race in the alternate hunter branch.

## Evolution requirements

Begin as Apex Hunter. Reach 2,000,000 base EP and defeat 5 different eligible boss types. Master at least one apex Unique skill as well. [Boss eligibility and counting](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Evolution#distinct-boss-kills).

## Racial attributes

| Attribute | Bonus at this stage |
| --- | ---: |
| Maximum health | +1,000 HP |
| Maximum spiritual health | +6,600 SHP |
| Attack damage | +6 |
| Movement speed | +15% |
| Knockback resistance | +0.3 |

These replace the previous hunter stage’s racial modifiers. They do not stack with earlier stages. Toggleable skill passives have separate modifiers and remain subject to attribute caps.

The HP bonuses across the branch are +100 / +500 / +1,000; SHP bonuses are +400 / +3,200 / +6,600. Each stage replaces the previous stage’s bonus. Existing hunter characters receive the new maximums on login without free healing.

## Skills and progression

This stage grants **[Sovereign Rebirth](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Aura-Regeneration)** as a native Extra Skill, at no permanent magicule acquisition cost. Earlier recovery tiers remain learned, but only the strongest toggled tier heals. Native racial skill learning enables the new tier automatically.

Base energy uses two thirds aura and one third magicules. Default-alignment kill EP retains Tensura’s native 2:1 split.


This stage unlocks the eligibility to evolve a mastered apex Unique into its matching Ultimate, provided you retain 2M base EP. Race evolution does not directly grant a Unique skill.

## Notes

Boss credit survives death. Old ordinary-hunt counts from 0.1.0 are not converted into boss credit. [Commands](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Commands) provide status and evolution controls.
