## Evolution

[Human hunter progression](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Evolution). Racial bonuses replace earlier stages; they do not stack.

## Racial ability

**Damage type:** None — the race grants a recovery Extra Skill, not an elemental attack.

This stage grants **[Sovereign Rebirth](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Sovereign-Rebirth)** as a native Extra Skill, at no permanent magicule acquisition cost. Earlier recovery tiers remain learned, but only the strongest toggled tier heals. Native racial skill learning enables the new tier automatically.

Base energy uses two thirds aura and one third magicules. Default-alignment kill EP retains Tensura’s native 2:1 split.


At 2M total EP, any race can combine two specific mastered Uniques through [Skill synthesis](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Skill-Synthesis). Race evolution does not directly grant a Unique skill.
