# Unique Skills

[Main Page](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Home) · [Skills](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Skills) · [Ability index](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Abilities) · [Damage types](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types)

**Unique Skills** are one of the addon’s skill tiers. All eight Uniques are rollable in Tensura’s configured reincarnation primary pool; no particular result is guaranteed.

## Skills

| Skill | Direct damage type | Active arts | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Storm Crown](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Storm-Crown) | [Lightning elemental](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types#lightning) | Railbolt, Thunder Step | Storm Mantle |
| [Scarlet Deluge](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Scarlet-Deluge) | [Water elemental](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types#water) | Crushing Current, Scarlet Undertow | Water Veil |
| [Black Flame](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Black-Flame) | [Fire elemental](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types#fire) | Oil Lash, Blackflame Ignition | Furnace Mantle |
| [Frostbound Colossus](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Frostbound-Colossus) | [Ice elemental](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Damage-Types#ice) | Avalanche Crash, Glacial Prison | Colossus Carapace |

| [Threadweaver](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Threadweaver) | Space elemental | Loom Needle, Sever Thread | Silken Instinct |
| [Rune Smith](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Rune-Smith) | Earth elemental | Runic Mine, Command Detonation | Runic Circuit |
| [Verdant Keeper](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Verdant-Keeper) | Earth elemental; healing has no damage type | Living Grove, Briar Grasp | Verdant Heart |
| [Mirrorbound](https://github.com/Bithio999/MC/wiki/Mirrorbound) | Light elemental; reflected shots retain their own type | Prismatic Lance, Mirror Aegis | Silvered Soul |

## Usage

Active modes consume aura. Toggleable passives consume ongoing aura and are separate from mode cycling. Each skill article lists costs, cooldowns, individual damage types, effects and limits.
