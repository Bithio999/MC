# Equipment

[All wikis](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Home) · [TensuraAdditionalAdditions](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/TensuraAdditionalAdditions)

[Inverted Spear of Heaven](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Inverted-Spear-of-Heaven) is a rare ominous-vault reward. Its [Nullification](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Nullification) engraving can attack spiritual bodies, shatter barriers and briefly seal skills.

[Chromatic Orb](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Chromatic-Orb) raises all eligible engravings by one level, beyond normal limits. Once per equipment piece; curses and blessings are excluded.


[Vesper](https://github.com/Bithio999/TensuraAdditionalAdditions/wiki/Additions-Vesper) — Kael’s unique silver sword with the Afterwind engraving.
