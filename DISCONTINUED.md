# Tensura Myths — discontinued

Tensura Myths is discontinued at **0.36.1**. Its source at the repository root, released builds and wiki remain archived for reference. Further features, bug fixes and compatibility updates are not planned.

**TensuraAdditionalAdditions is the active project**, in [`standalone/tensura-additional-additions`](standalone/tensura-additional-additions). It is a separate distribution, rather than a full replacement for every Myths feature. Do not install both in one instance.
